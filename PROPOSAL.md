# Corte 1 Project Proposal

For the Corte 1 conversational assistant project, I will build an AI assistant focused entirely on the documentation and research context of my graduation project ("proyecto de grado"). The assistant will use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over the core thesis documents, theoretical framework, and technical requirements. It is designed to help advisors, evaluators, and myself quickly consult specific details, methodologies, and findings without having to manually search through the extensive project documentation. By integrating this course's requirements with my graduation project, I can directly apply multi-agent orchestration and LLM fundamentals to a real-world academic deliverable.

## Example Questions
Once RAG is integrated in Week 4, the assistant should be able to answer questions such as:
1. "What is the primary methodology used in the project, and why was it chosen over alternative approaches?"
2. "According to the theoretical framework chapter, what are the main constraints or limitations identified?"
3. "Summarize the technical requirements for the deployment phase as specified in the architecture document."
